What this is

Electronics, taught like it's actually hard, because it is

Tronic Zenergy is a structured electronics curriculum built around one idea: you should be able to go from "what is a resistor" to "I built a working 4-bit computer" without ever hitting a gap where nobody explained the step in between.

Every module is recorded, in order, by one person teaching, with no padding and no assumption that you already know the jargon. You watch, you build on your own breadboard alongside it, you sit a short exam that checks it actually landed, and you move on to the next module once you've proven it.

This site also doubles as a build log. Every project Tronic Zenergy publishes, from a two-transistor flasher to the full custom CPU, lives in the Projects section with the schematic and the reasoning behind it, not just the finished result.
