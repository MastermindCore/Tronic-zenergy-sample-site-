Curriculum

Seven modules, beginner to expert

Each module unlocks once you've paid for it, and the levels build on each other: Logic Gates assumes you already know Ohm's law, the 4-bit computer assumes you already know how an adder works. Pay per module, or work straight through the whole path.

How it works

From sign-up to certificate

1. Create a free account, then enroll in a module. Pay by card or M-Pesa. Access opens as soon as payment confirms.

2. Watch the lessons and build alongside them. Your dashboard tracks which lessons you've finished.

3. Sit the module exam. Score 70% or higher to pass. You can retake it if you don't.

4. Get your certificate, and book time with Ezra. Once you've purchased a module, you can schedule a weekly 1-on-1 Q&A session to go over what you learned.
