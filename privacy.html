This is a starting structure, not legal advice: because students join from around the world, have it reviewed against the data protection rules that apply to you (Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, and the GDPR if you accept students from the EU or UK).

1. Who this covers

This policy explains how Tronic Zenergy ("we", "us") collects, uses, and stores information about people who visit this site, create an account, or enroll in a class ("you"). Tronic Zenergy is the data controller for this information.

2. What we collect

Account details you give us: name, email address, and password (stored only as a hash by our authentication provider, never as plain text).

Learning activity: which modules you've purchased, which lessons you've marked complete, exam scores and attempts, and certificates issued.

1-on-1 session requests: the day, time, and note you submit when you request a Q&A session, plus your name and email so we can confirm the booking.

Community posts: the title, caption, and image link you submit to the community wall, shown publicly with your name once approved.

Payment records: the transaction reference, amount, and status for each purchase. Payments are handled by our payment processor, Flutterwave. We do not receive or store your full card number or M-Pesa PIN.

Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, and basic usage logs, collected automatically for security and to keep the site working.

3. Why we collect it

To create and secure your account, unlock the modules you've paid for, track your progress and issue certificates, schedule 1-on-1 sessions, moderate the community wall, process and reconcile payments, answer support requests, and meet our own legal and tax record-keeping obligations.

4. Who we share it with

Flutterwave, to process card and mobile money payments including M-Pesa. Google Firebase, which hosts our authentication, database, and certificate storage. YouTube, which hosts and plays the lesson videos embedded on the site: when you watch a lesson, YouTube may collect data under its own privacy policy. Google Meet or Zoom, if you book a 1-on-1 session, to run the video call. We do not sell your data to advertisers or other third parties, and we never show your exam results or purchases to other students.

5. Where your data is processed

Because our providers operate internationally, your information may be processed in countries other than the one you live in. We choose providers that apply recognized data protection safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

Account and learning data for as long as your account is active, plus [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 3 years] afterward for support and dispute purposes. Payment records are kept for [RETENTION PERIOD] to meet tax and accounting requirements.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm what data we hold about you, correct it, delete it (subject to our legal retention obligations), or give you a copy of it. To make a request, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL].

8. Security

Access to your data is restricted by Firebase security rules to you and to our administrators, and payment and exam grading run on the server rather than in your browser. No system is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's data
